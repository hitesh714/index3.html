What We Do

» About » What We Do

The Go Green Intiative works to improve lifelong outcomes for children in communities most impacted by environmental harm by advancing environmental health, safety, and sustainability at school. We work in partnership with district administrators, school, stakeholders, and student leaders to improve the quality of te air, water, and food at school while conserving school and community resources.

How We Work

Our five coore programs guide, train, and support school officials, staff, and students in improving indoor environmental conditions, conserving natural resources, and reducing environmental impacts.
